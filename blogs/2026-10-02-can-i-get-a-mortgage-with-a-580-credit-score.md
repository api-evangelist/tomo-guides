Key Takeaways What the Federal Housing Administration (FHA) permits at a 580 credit score FHA loans are built around the 580 credit score line. In fiscal year 2025, the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD) insured 87,846 FHA loans for borrowers with credit scores from 580 to 619.
