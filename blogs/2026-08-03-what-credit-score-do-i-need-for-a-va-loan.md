The Department of Veterans Affairs sets no minimum credit score for a VA loan. That requirement comes from individual lenders. Lenders add their own credit score rules on top of the VA’s guidelines because they still carry the risk if a borrower defaults on the part of the loan the VA doesn’t guarantee.
