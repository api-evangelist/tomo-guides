The term mortgage points (also called discount points) gets frequently thrown around, but what do points actually mean and should you get them? Quick summary: Mortgage discount points So what are points anyway? Mortgage “points,” also referred to as discount points, are upfront fees paid to your lender at the time of closing on your property.
